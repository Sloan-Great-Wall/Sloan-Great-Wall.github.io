Privacy Policy — INS Go Sight Capture

Last updated 24 August 2026 · Magipop

INS Go Sight Capture is a field tool for walking street-view surveys. It records a walking route and lets you photograph what you pass, storing sensor readings taken at the moment of each shot.

Everything the app records stays on your device. The app contains no networking code: it cannot and does not send your photos, your route, or any sensor reading to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no login, and no analytics.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Camera — to take the survey photographs you choose to take. Photos are written to the app's own storage on your device.
  • Location, including in the background — to record the route you walk and to geotag each photo. Background access exists so the route stays continuous when the screen locks or you switch apps during an active session. Location is only recorded while a session you started is running.
  • Motion & orientation — to record device attitude, heading and gyroscope readings at the shutter moment, so a photo can later be related to the direction it was taken in.

Where the data lives

Captured photos, route points and sensor snapshots are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not backed up to any service by the app itself. Deleting a session inside the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

Sharing

The app can export a session as a .zip file through the standard iOS share sheet. That export happens only when you ask for it, and it goes wherever you send it. We never receive it.

Children

The app is a professional survey tool and is not directed at children.
